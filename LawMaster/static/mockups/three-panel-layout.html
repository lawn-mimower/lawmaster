Under Section 41A read with the Second Schedule of the Factories Act, 1948, the permissible exposure limits for Benzene are:

Time-Weighted Average (8 hrs): 10 ppm / 30 mg/m³ 1
Short-Term Exposure Limit (15 min): 25 ppm / 75 mg/m³ 1

Benzene is classified as a hazardous substance under the Second Schedule. The occupier must ensure that no worker is exposed beyond these limits, and must arrange for periodic medical examinations under Section 41E. 2

Sources: 1 Factories Act 1948, Second Schedule, p.55   2 Factories Act 1948, Section 41E, p.27
